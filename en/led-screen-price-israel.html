What Affects the Price of an LED Screen?

  • Pixel pitch — the smaller the number, the denser the pixels and the higher the price per square meter. P2.5 suits close viewing indoors, P4–P5 suits a lobby, and malls run anywhere from P2.5 to P8 depending on the corridor.
  • Screen size — priced per square meter, so double the area is roughly double the price.
  • Indoor vs. outdoor — an outdoor screen needs IP65 sealing and 5,000–6,000 nits, so it costs more than an indoor screen of the same size.
  • Installation complexity — height, drilling into concrete and a dedicated power line all add to the cost.
  • Software — player and Pixel CMS Starter are included in year one; afterwards ₪69 per screen per month + VAT, or ₪149 on the Business plan.
