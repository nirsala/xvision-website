What Is a Transparent LED Screen?

A transparent LED screen arranges its LEDs in thin rows with clear gaps between them. When it is on you see video; when it is off you see through it to whatever is behind — up to 95% transparency according to the manufacturer's specification. The film version is 1.8 mm thick and weighs 6 kg per square meter, so an ordinary shop window carries it without any frame or structure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a transparent LED screen?

An LED screen whose LEDs are arranged in thin rows with clear gaps between them. When it is on you see video, and when it is off you see through it, with up to 95% transparency according to the manufacturer's specification.

Can it be stuck onto my existing window?

Yes. The transparent LED film is adhered to the inside of the existing glass, including curved glass. It is 1.8 mm thick and weighs 6 kg per square meter, so no supporting structure is needed.

Is the image visible in daylight?

Yes. Brightness is 5,000 nits, enough for a shop window facing the street. The screen is designed for indoor use or behind glass (IP20).

What is the difference between P2.5 and P6.25?

The number is the distance in millimeters between one LED and the next. P2.5 is sharper and suits close viewing, for example inside a store. P6.25 is more transparent and suits large fronts viewed from a distance.

How much does a transparent LED screen cost?
