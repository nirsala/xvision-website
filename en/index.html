About Pixel by Keshet

Founded in 2018, Pixel by Keshet is Israel's leading supplier of indoor and outdoor LED screens for businesses, retail chains, hotels, municipalities, and event venues. With 100+ installed projects across Israel and a dedicated support team of 12 professionals, we deliver complete digital signage solutions from supply to installation to content management.

We import LED panels from certified tier-1 manufacturers and provide everything in-house: site survey, engineering, installation, content management system (CMS) in Hebrew, staff training, and ongoing maintenance. Our clients range from boutique cafes to national retail chains to government municipalities.

Mission: Making professional digital signage accessible to every business in Israel — with competitive pricing, full transparency, and service that actually works.
