What Is a Digital Signage CMS?

A digital signage CMS is the software layer that decides what plays on your screens — and when, where, and to whom. Instead of walking up to each screen with a USB stick, you manage everything from one web dashboard: upload media, build playlists, schedule content by time of day and by location, and push updates to a single screen or a thousand screens at once.

The hardware — the LED panel — is only half the story. The CMS is what turns a screen into a marketing and communication tool that actually earns its keep. A weak CMS means content that never changes; a strong one means the right message on the right screen at the right moment, with almost no manual effort.

The Pixel by Keshet CMS is built specifically for the Israeli market — a full Hebrew interface with right-to-left support, English alongside it, and the option to run entirely on your own servers.

Why a Locally-Built CMS Beats a Generic Global Platform

Global platforms like the well-known cloud signage tools are capable — but they are built for an English, left-to-right world. In Israel that creates real friction: Hebrew text that breaks, right-to-left layouts that misalign, support in a different time zone, and data that lives on servers you don't control.

Data Privacy: The Self-Hosted Advantage

Most digital signage platforms are cloud-only — your content, schedules and (increasingly) audience data pass through servers owned by a third party abroad. For many organizations in Israel — municipalities, financial institutions, healthcare, security-sensitive businesses — that's a non-starter.

We offer a self-hosted deployment on dedicated servers, so every asset and every data point stays inside your environment and never leaves it. It's the same standard we apply across the digital infrastructures our team runs for major Israeli organizations.
