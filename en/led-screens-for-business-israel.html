Why an LED Screen Pays Off for a Business

An LED screen replaces static printed signs with content you can change in seconds — promotions, prices, videos and product information — from anywhere. A printed sign needs a new print run every time the message changes; a screen is a one-time investment that keeps working for years. Moving content also draws more attention than a sign customers have already stopped noticing, and a store with a screen in the window can change its message several times a day at no extra cost.

Types of LED Screens for Business

1. Indoor LED screens

For covered spaces — stores, restaurants, hotel and office lobbies, malls and sports halls. They come at high resolution (P2.5 for close viewing, P4–P5 in a lobby) and 800–1,500 nits, which is plenty under indoor lighting. Sizes run from 1×1 m at a checkout up to 6×4 m and beyond.

2. Shop-window screens

A screen behind the glass of a shop window has to compete with daylight, so it needs 2,000–4,000 nits. If you want shoppers to keep seeing into the store, a transparent LED screen sticks to the existing glass with up to 95% transparency.

3. Outdoor LED screens and digital billboards

Built for rain, heat and dust: IP65 sealing and 5,000–6,000 nits for full visibility in direct sun. Suited to shop fronts, main roads, parking lots and stadiums. Outdoor signs usually also need a municipal signage permit, which we help with.

4. Lobby and corridor screens

Portrait or landscape screens in lobbies, elevators and corridors, showing visitor information, internal announcements and schedules — common in hotels, office buildings and shopping centers.
