Indoor vs. Outdoor — Choosing Right

The single most common mistake is buying the wrong class of screen for the environment. Here's the short version:

FactorIndoor LEDOutdoor LED
Pixel pitchFine (P1.5–P4)Coarser (P4–P10)
Brightness800–1,500 nits5,000+ nits (sun-readable)
ProtectionStandardIP65 — rain & dust sealed
Best forClose viewing, interiorsStreets, storefronts, distance

Get the viewing distance and environment right and the screen looks flawless for years; get it wrong and no CMS or content can save it. That's exactly what the site survey is for.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an LED screen supplier in Israel actually provide?

A full-service supplier handles the whole chain: site survey, panel and pixel-pitch selection, importing certified hardware, mounting and installation, CMS setup, staff training and ongoing maintenance. We deliver all of it in-house rather than sub-contracting it out.

What is the difference between indoor and outdoor LED screens?

Indoor screens use a fine pixel pitch and moderate brightness for close viewing; outdoor screens are IP65-rated and reach 5,000+ nits to stay readable in direct sun. The right choice depends on viewing distance and environment.

How do I choose the best LED screen company in Israel?

Pick a supplier that owns the full process end to end, imports from certified tier-1 manufacturers, provides a local Hebrew & English CMS, offers a real warranty, and gives same-time-zone support. A transparent, itemized quote is a strong signal.
