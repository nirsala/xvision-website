LED Wall vs. Video Wall: Is There a Difference?

The terms are often used interchangeably, and in practice they refer to the same product category: a large-format display assembled from modular LED panels. Some industry professionals use "LED wall" to describe a single contiguous installation, while "video wall" can also describe LCD multi-panel arrays. For the purposes of this guide — and for most Israeli buyers — an LED wall means a seamless modular LED display built from individual cabinets, scalable to any dimension.

Key distinguishing features of a true LED wall:

  • Modular panel construction — any size, any shape, including curved, cylindrical, or convex layouts
  • Self-emissive pixels — each LED generates its own light; no backlight needed
  • High brightness output — 800 to 8,000 nits depending on the application
  • Wide viewing angle — typically 160° horizontal, ensuring clear visibility across large spaces
  • Long service life — 100,000+ hours at specified brightness levels

Indoor LED Wall vs. Outdoor LED Wall in Israel

The Israeli climate presents specific challenges for outdoor LED installations. Summer temperatures regularly exceed 35°C, direct desert-origin sunlight is intense, and coastal areas expose displays to salt air. Choosing an LED wall rated for the wrong environment is one of the most common and costly mistakes buyers make.

Indoor LED walls are optimized for brightness levels between 800 and 2,000 nits — enough to look stunning in artificially lit lobbies, showrooms, and auditoriums without drawing excessive power or generating unnecessary heat. Cabinet cooling is typically passive or fan-based with quieter operation suited for occupied indoor environments.

Outdoor LED walls require IP65 or higher weather protection, active cooling or heat-dissipating cabinet design, anti-UV coatings, and brightness levels of 4,000–8,000 nits to overpower direct Israeli sunlight. Sizing and Aspect Ratio: Getting It Right

Unlike a standard TV purchase where you choose from fixed sizes, an LED wall is custom-built to your dimensions. This flexibility is powerful but requires planning. Consider:

  • Viewing distance: the audience's typical distance from the screen determines the pixel pitch and therefore the minimum practical resolution
  • Content aspect ratio: standard 16:9 for video content, but LED walls can be wide-format 32:9, portrait, or custom for architectural installations
  • Structural constraints: wall weight capacity, ceiling clearance, electrical load, and cable routing all affect installation planning
  • Surrounding aesthetics: the LED wall should complement the architecture, not fight it — our design team provides renders before fabrication begins
