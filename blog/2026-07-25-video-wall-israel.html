What Is a Video Wall and Why Do Israeli Businesses Choose LED?

A video wall is a multi-screen array — typically built from individual LED panels — that functions as one seamless display. Unlike a standard large-format TV, an LED video wall can be configured in virtually any shape or size, scaled from a modest 2×2 grid to a massive curved installation spanning an entire lobby wall.

Israeli businesses choose LED over LCD for three compelling reasons. First, no visible bezels: modern fine-pitch LED panels join edge-to-edge, creating a single uninterrupted canvas. Second, brightness: we specify 800–1,500 nits indoors, 2,000–4,000 nits behind glass and 5,000–6,000 nits outdoors, so the wall stays readable in brightly lit showrooms and sun-drenched plazas alike. Third, serviceability: a video wall is built from modules, so a fault means swapping one module — we replace a faulty module within 48 hours anywhere in Israel — rather than replacing a whole screen.

Choosing the Right Pixel Pitch for Your Space

Pixel pitch — the distance in millimeters between individual LED pixels — determines optimal viewing distance and sharpness. Choosing incorrectly means paying too much for resolution you can't appreciate, or too little for a display that looks grainy at the distances your audience actually stands.

As a practical rule of thumb: multiply the pixel pitch by 1 meter to get the minimum comfortable viewing distance. Our standard specifications follow that logic — P2.5 for meeting rooms and other close-proximity indoor applications like a reception counter or museum exhibit, P4–P5 for building lobbies, and P2.5–P8 across shopping-mall environments depending on whether viewers stand at a storefront or look across an atrium.

Pixel by Keshet's technical team evaluates your specific space — room dimensions, ambient light, audience viewing distances, and content type — before recommending a pixel pitch, so you never overspend or underperform.
