What Is a Video Wall and Why Do Israeli Businesses Choose LED?

A video wall is a multi-screen array — typically built from individual LED panels — that functions as one seamless display. Unlike a standard large-format TV, an LED video wall can be configured in virtually any shape or size, scaled from a modest 2×2 grid to a massive curved installation spanning an entire lobby wall.

Israeli businesses choose LED over LCD for three compelling reasons. First, no visible bezels: modern fine-pitch LED panels join edge-to-edge, creating a single uninterrupted canvas. Second, brightness: LED panels achieve 1,000–6,000 nits, making them clearly visible even in brightly lit showrooms or sun-drenched outdoor plazas. Third, longevity: a quality LED video wall runs 100,000+ hours before reaching half brightness — typically more than a decade of continuous use.

Choosing the Right Pixel Pitch for Your Space

Pixel pitch — the distance in millimeters between individual LED pixels — determines optimal viewing distance and sharpness. Choosing incorrectly means paying too much for resolution you can't appreciate, or too little for a display that looks grainy at the distances your audience actually stands.

As a practical rule of thumb: multiply the pixel pitch by 1 meter to get the minimum comfortable viewing distance. A P2.5 panel (2.5mm pitch) delivers sharp imagery from 2.5 meters away — ideal for close-proximity indoor applications like a reception counter or museum exhibit. A P4 or P6 panel suits lobbies and event stages where viewers stand 4–8 meters back. Outdoor installations on building facades typically use P8–P16 because distances are much greater and direct sunlight exposure calls for robustness over fine detail.
