Indoor vs. Outdoor LED: What Changes When You Go Inside

Indoor LED displays operate in a fundamentally different environment from their outdoor counterparts, and the specifications reflect this. Brightness requirements are lower: we specify 800–1,500 nits for a normally lit interior, 2,000–4,000 nits for a screen facing a shop window, and 5,000–6,000 nits only for a screen that works outdoors. Lower brightness enables a finer pixel pitch at the same budget — P2.5 is our default indoor pitch for close viewing, and it delivers a seamless image from about 2.5 metres away.

Thermal conditions also shift. Israeli commercial interiors are heavily air-conditioned in summer, creating thermal cycling as staff open and close doors. Indoor LED panels need adequate thermal design, but they do not require the IP65-rated enclosures and active ventilation systems mandatory for outdoor units. The result is slimmer, lighter panels that can be flush-mounted, recessed, or suspended without structural complexity.

How to Specify an Indoor LED Display: The Four Critical Decisions

Getting these four decisions right determines whether your indoor LED installation delivers on its promise:

1. Pixel Pitch

Divide the minimum viewing distance (in metres) by 1,000 — that gives you the maximum pixel pitch in millimetres at which the image will appear seamless. A viewer 3 metres from the screen can tolerate roughly P3; a viewer 2 metres away cannot. In practice we specify P2.5 for a meeting room, P4–P5 for a lobby, and P2.5–P8 across a mall depending on where the nearest viewer stands. Over-specifying (too fine a pitch) wastes budget without visible quality gain; under-specifying produces a visible dot-matrix appearance at normal viewing distance.

2. Screen Size and Format

Indoor LED panels are modular — they tile seamlessly to any width and height. Standard cabinet sizes of 500×500mm or 1,000×500mm allow custom aspect ratios impossible with fixed-size commercial displays. This matters for Israeli retail environments where shopfront widths rarely conform to standard 16:9 proportions.

3. Content Management System

The CMS is where your investment lives day-to-day. For Israeli businesses, mandatory requirements include: full Hebrew RTL support, cloud-based scheduling (no on-premise server dependency), mobile update capability, and multi-zone layout support for screens running simultaneous news tickers, product information, and branded backgrounds. 4. Service and Warranty

LED panels are field-serviceable at module level — individual modules can be replaced without removing the entire screen. A credible Israeli supplier should offer: on-site warranty service, spare module inventory held locally (not overseas), and remote diagnostics. Total Cost of Ownership: Beyond the Purchase Price

Indoor LED screens have very low running costs compared to traditional display technologies. LED panels consume less power than fluorescent-backlit displays of equivalent size and require no lamp replacement. What you can actually hold a supplier to is the warranty and the service commitment, so factor the purchase price, installation, the Pixel CMS subscription, power and maintenance into one sheet before comparing quotations.

An installed indoor screen at P2.5 starts at ₪3,000–5,000 for 1 × 1 m and ₪6,000–8,000 for 2 × 1 m. A 3 × 2 m wall is ₪18,000–24,000, a 4 × 2 m wall is ₪24,000–30,000, and 6 × 4 m is ₪72,000–84,000. Per square metre that is about ₪3,000–5,000 on small screens, falling to roughly ₪3,000–3,500 on large ones.

What brightness does an indoor LED display need?

800–1,500 nits is right for a normally lit interior. A screen facing a shop window needs 2,000–4,000 nits because it competes with daylight through the glass, and only a screen that actually works outdoors needs 5,000–6,000 nits with an IP65 rating.

Which pixel pitch should I choose for a meeting room or a lobby?

P2.5 for a meeting room, where the nearest viewer sits about 2–3 metres from the screen. P4–P5 for a lobby, where people look at the wall from further back. Across a mall the range is P2.5–P8, chosen by where the nearest viewer actually stands.
